JobScout privacy policy

JobScout is a personal tool used only by its owner. It is not offered to the public.

Google user data

JobScout connects only to its owner's own Gmail account. It uses Gmail access to send emails the owner has authorised and to read the threads of those emails, so it can detect replies and bounces.

This data is stored on the owner's own computer. It is not sold, used for advertising, or used to train AI models, and it is not shared with anyone except as needed to deliver the emails themselves.

JobScout's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

People who are emailed

JobScout stores the name, title, company, work email address and public profile link of recruiters and hiring contacts, on the owner's own computer, to manage job search outreach. Anyone who asks not to be contacted is added to a permanent do-not-contact list.

Contact

Reach the owner through GitHub, or by replying to any email JobScout sent you.
